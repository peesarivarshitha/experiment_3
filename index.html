Vignan Institute of Technology & Science, also known as VITS is a private college established in 1999 in Hyderabad, affiliated to JNTUH and approved by AICTE and NBA. The college has been ranked 151-200 by NIRF, 100 by India Today under the engineering category in 2024, and 74th by Times of India under the engineering category in 2025.
